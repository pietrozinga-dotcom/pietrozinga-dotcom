## About

Founder of [Trinity Digital Studio](https://www.trinitydigitalstudio.it) in Turin. We ship **websites, web apps, mobile apps, e-commerce, and custom gestionali** for companies that run crews, calendars, and back-office work every day.

Two products in production or late delivery: a brokerage back-office with an insurance agency, and a field-ops system (web + app) for window and door installation.

## Work

**Brokerage operations** — private, with an insurance agency  
Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase.

- Inbound email becomes a deal record instead of a inbox-to-spreadsheet loop
- Documents extracted into structured fields, not retyped
- Roles and GDPR-aware handling of client data

**Installation field ops** — private, web + dedicated mobile app  
For a company that mounts and unmounts window and door fittings.

- Jobs, appointments, and on-site work (not warehouse stock)
- Office on the dashboard, crews on the app
- One flow from booking the pose to closing the cantiere
